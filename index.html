ENGINEERING CONTEXT

Critical competition requirements

Four constraints drive the chassis, launcher and attachment-system design.

Stowed size
600 mm

Maximum 600 × 600 × 600 mm envelope, excluding the modular launcher.

Robot mass
36 kg

Maximum without launcher; 41 kg with detachable launcher attached.

Chassis power
50 W

Strict locomotion power cap, motivating efficient wheel-leg mobility.

Launcher module
42 mm

Detachable projectile launcher collected and attached during the match.

THE DESIGN APPROACH

Three coupled mechanical systems

The report documents how the subsystems were compared, selected, and prepared for validation.

01

Wheel-leg chassis

Reuse the team's existing serial-leg assemblies within a new crossed chassis structure designed around the 200 mm step.

Architecture rationale →
02

Launcher acquisition

Evaluate arms, gantry mechanisms and linear pullers. A timing-belt puller is the current provisional selection.

Concept evaluation →
03

Modular launcher

Assess module boundaries to reduce detachable weight and connection complexity without compromising feeding reliability.
